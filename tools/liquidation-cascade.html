How the amplification works

Three prices do three different jobs: the last trade sets the index, the mark sets margin, and the book sets execution. When a leveraged position is liquidated, the exchange sends a market order, not a limit order. That order consumes the book, moves the mark, and triggers the next tier of liquidation — which is why the size of the initial move rarely matches the size of the print. The cascade ends only when the book refills or the liquidations run out.
