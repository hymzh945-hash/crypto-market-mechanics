Why this matters

0.01% per 8 hours looks like nothing. It is 0.03% a day, about 0.9% a month, and on a strategy that trades a 0.15% edge it can turn a positive expectancy into a negative one within two weeks. Funding is the only cost in crypto that scales with time instead of volume, which is why a position that is right on direction can still lose.
