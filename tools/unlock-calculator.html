Token unlock impact calculator

Every unlock is a supply shock. This estimates the price the market has to clear at, and how early it typically starts pricing it in.

Current float, not FDV.

Absolute amount, or use the slider below.

Float before / after10.0% → 12.0%
New supply vs old supply+20.0%
Price has to clear at$0.833 per $1.00
Market usually starts pricing this in14–26 days out

Model: price scales with 1/supply, demand held flat. In practice the decline is larger and earlier, for two reasons: unlock recipients have a very low cost basis and will sell at almost any price, and the market prices the event in ahead of time rather than on the day.

The rule that follows: cut size two weeks before an unlock, and wait three days after it before deciding anything. Do not buy the dip on unlock day.
