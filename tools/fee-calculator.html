The break-even number is the important one. If your average move is smaller than twice the fee, you are paying the exchange to move money around. This is why fee tiers are arithmetic rather than opinion — and why "I was right but I still lost" is usually a fee statement, not a market statement.

The rule that follows: size your trades so that the expected move is at least 5× the round-trip fee. Below that, the fee is the strategy.
